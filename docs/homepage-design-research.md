# Homepage design research (2026-10-24)

Sites reviewed in a browser, plus 2026 law-firm design roundups. Agency
roundups showcase their own clients, so they were used for recurring themes,
not rankings.

## What the sites do

**Kirkland & Ellis** (kirkland.com)
- Dark, full-screen panels; each scroll brings a new featured story.
- Serif headlines, a small category label, one large image per panel.
- Header is almost empty: logo, search, menu.

**Latham & Watkins** (lw.com)
- Light and calm; rankings stated as a large italic sentence.
- Imagery as soft architectural texture rather than people.

**Morgan & Morgan** (forthepeople.com), closest to our audience
- Phone number very large, top right, in every header state.
- Hero = the claim + "free case evaluation" as the first action.
- Directly under the hero: three short, bold value statements
  ("The fee is free, unless we win") with a short accent rule above each.
- "As seen on" proof strip early on the page.

**Stripe** (stripe.com), motion and polish reference
- Two-tone headline: the second half in a lighter tone.
- A live, ticking figure in the hero.
- Trust logos in a strip immediately under the hero.
- Product tiles that show the real thing; motion responds to scroll.

## Recurring themes from the roundups
- Trust within seconds: does this firm handle my case, who will I talk to.
- One clear consultation action near the top and again at the end.
- Proof (results, reviews, awards) where the eye lands, not buried.
- Humanize the attorney; bios beyond a credential list.
- Practice areas visible from the homepage, each leading to a page.

## Applied to jjlawohio.com home

1. Trust strip directly under the hero (Stripe, Morgan & Morgan): Google 4.9,
   BBB A+, NCDD member, BusinessRate Best of 2026, Ohio Bar.
2. Three value statements under it (Morgan & Morgan): former prosecutor,
   direct access to the attorney, clear fees.
3. Two-tone hero headline (Stripe): already "Defending Your Rights." in
   white, "Building Your Future." in sky.
4. Live elements (Stripe's ticker): office status and the court finder.
5. Practice areas as large image panels that stack as you scroll
   (Kirkland's full-screen panels), using the firm's own practice photos.
6. Real figures that count up on view: 4.9 rating, 100+ reviews, 11 courts,
   licensed 2019. Server HTML always holds the final numbers.
7. Phone always reachable: a call bar on phones after the hero.
8. Motion clearly visible but calm: word-by-word hero reveal, stacking
   panels, count-ups, image reveals, struck-through charges, hover states.
   All of it off under reduced motion.
