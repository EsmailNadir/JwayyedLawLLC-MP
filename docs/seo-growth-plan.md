# SEO growth plan: Jwayyed Law LLC

Goal: rank in Columbus and across the served counties for OVI/DUI first, then criminal defense. Built from the seo-audit, seo-local, link-building and ai-seo playbooks. The site itself is now in good shape (every page passes the on-page audit, `scripts/seo-live-audit.py`); the gains from here are mostly off-site: the Google Business Profile, reviews, consistent listings and earned links.

Rule for everything below: no bought links, link exchanges, private blog networks or automated link tools. Google treats those as spam. Any paid placement must carry `rel="nofollow"` or `rel="sponsored"`.

## Week 1: launch (about 2 hours)

2. **Launch**, then in Search Console: Sitemaps > resubmit `https://www.jjlawohio.com/sitemap.xml`. Use URL Inspection > Request indexing for: `/`, `/ovi-dui-defense`, `/ovi-dui-defense/first-ovi`, `/criminal-defense`, `/locations/franklin-county`, `/contact`, `/courts`, and the six new OVI blog posts.
3. **Check the robots fix took effect**: open `https://www.jjlawohio.com/robots.txt`; it must not list `/_next/`. Then run the homepage through Search Console's URL Inspection > Test live URL > View tested page > Screenshot; it should look styled.
4. **Run Google's Rich Results Test** on `/ovi-dui-defense/first-ovi` (FAQ and breadcrumb data should be detected).

## Week 1 to 2: Google Business Profile (the biggest local lever)

The profile drives the map results ("OVI lawyer near me"). In the profile:

- **Primary category**: the closest OVI/DUI or criminal defense category Google offers (search "DUI" and "criminal" in the category picker). Add the other practice areas as secondary categories (personal injury, estate planning, business). The wrong primary category is the most damaging local mistake.
- **Hours**: Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. Same as the site.
- **Website link**: the homepage, or `/contact`.
- **Services**: add each practice area with a one-line description, OVI first.
- **Description**: plain, factual, mention Columbus, OVI/DUI, criminal defense, former prosecutor. **Do not say "free consultation"** (the firm does not offer one).
- **Photos**: the studio headshot, the office building and suite entrance, the Best of 2026 plaque. Add a few new ones each month.
- **Posts**: one a week linking to a blog post or practice page (the new OVI posts are ready-made).
- **Questions**: answer common questions on the website (done: every page has FAQs) rather than in the profile.

## Ongoing: reviews

- Ask every client at the end of a matter, with the direct Google review link. Steady new reviews matter more than the total.
- Reply to every review. **Never confirm or discuss case details in a reply** (attorney-client confidentiality). A thank-you is enough.
- Never offer anything in exchange for a review and never screen who is asked (Google and FTC rules).
- Ask some clients to review on Avvo too: AI assistants pull from several review sites.

## Ongoing: earned links (the link-building playbook)

The site already has linkable assets other sites have a reason to cite:

- the **court guides** (`/courts`): address, schedule and what each court hears
- the **free document templates** (`/our-law-firm/document-templates`)
- the **OVI and record-sealing explainers** in the blog
- the **Athens students OVI article**

Highest-yield targets, in order:

1. **Unlinked mentions.** Search Google for "Jwayyed Law" -site:jjlawohio.com. Wherever the firm is named without a link (for example the BusinessRate award page, event listings, news), ask for a link to the homepage.
2. **Local resource pages.** Campus student legal services and student guides (Ohio State, Ohio University in Athens, Columbus State, Capital), public library legal-help pages, re-entry and record-sealing resource lists, driver-safety and treatment-program resource pages. Pitch the court guide or the relevant explainer, not the homepage.
3. **Expert quotes.** Sign up for journalist-request services (Qwoted, Featured, Connectively) and answer Ohio OVI, traffic and criminal law questions. Local news (Columbus Dispatch, local TV) runs OVI stories around holidays: send a short note before Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day and New Year's offering comment on Ohio OVI law and HB 37.
4. **Bar and alumni publications.** An article in an OSBA or CBA publication, or a Capital Law alumni feature, earns authoritative links and supports his expertise signals.
5. **Podcasts and local talks.** Local business or community podcasts; talks at community groups. Ask for a link on the episode page.

## How to measure (weekly, 15 minutes)

- Search Console > Performance: clicks and impressions for queries containing "ovi", "dui", "columbus", "lawyer", "attorney"; compare to the baseline export.
- Search Console > Pages: any new errors or pages dropping out of the index.
- Google Business Profile > Performance: calls, website clicks and direction requests.
- Count of Google reviews this month (target: steady, every month).
- Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Mode "best OVI lawyer in Columbus Ohio" monthly; note whether the firm is cited and which pages they cite.
